FamilyPause

FamilyPause is a web app for families. You type, paste, or record what is going on this week. FamilyPause turns that into a shared plan of events, reminders, and tasks.

You review each item and choose what to keep. After you approve an item, FamilyPause can create or update a matching event on the Google Calendar you connect. FamilyPause does not read your existing calendar to build the plan. Calendar access is optional. You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Settings.
