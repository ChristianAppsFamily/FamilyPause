Privacy Policy

Last updated August 31, 2026

FamilyPause helps families organize their week by turning conversations, notes, and transcripts into a shared plan that auto-syncs to their calendar. Your family's conversations are private. They always will be.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and first name. When you use FamilyPause we collect the content you provide, typed notes, pasted text, or recorded speech transcribed to text, along with the family member names, business names, and categories you add to your workspace. If you connect Google Calendar we store an access token, a refresh token, and the Google account email address associated with that connection. We also collect basic usage data such as session counts and feature interactions to improve the product.

How we use it

Your content is used for one purpose: to organize your week. Transcripts and notes you provide are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to extract appointments, tasks, reminders, and decisions. The extracted items are returned to your workspace and nothing else. We do not use your family's conversations to train AI models. We do not sell your data. We do not advertise to you based on your content. FamilyPause is and will always be ad-free.

Google Calendar integration

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, FamilyPause accesses the following Google user data through the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens used to call Google Calendar on your behalf
  • The Google account email for the connected account (so you can see which account is linked in Settings)
  • Calendar events that FamilyPause creates, updates, or deletes for plan items you explicitly approve

We use this Google user data only to sync approved FamilyPause plan items to your Google Calendar and to maintain that connection. We do not read your existing calendar events, free/busy information, or other calendars beyond what that scope allows for managing events FamilyPause creates. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use it for advertising, credit, lending, or resale. We do not use Google Workspace or Google Calendar API data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Google OAuth tokens and Google Calendar event data are not shared with Anthropic, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. They are used only with Google's Calendar API, except when disclosure is required by law. You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from Settings. Disconnecting removes our stored tokens and the connected Google account email from our systems. Events already created in your Google Calendar remain in Google Calendar unless you delete them there.

While Google Calendar stays connected, we retain tokens only as long as needed to keep sync working. If you request deletion of your FamilyPause account or workspace (see below), we delete stored Google tokens and related connection data as part of that request. The use of raw or derived user data received from Google APIs (including Google Calendar / Workspace APIs) adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site traffic, such as which pages are visited and how often. It is configured so it does not receive form entries, conversations, calendar information, names, email addresses, or other personal data. Page paths are sent without query strings. We do not use analytics for advertising.

Data storage and sharing

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure with encryption at rest. Data in transit is protected with HTTPS. Google OAuth tokens are stored with the same protections and are not sent to Anthropic. Workspace data, your plans, family member names, and session content, is visible only to members of your workspace. We share user-provided notes and transcripts with Anthropic solely for AI extraction under their privacy terms. We use Google Analytics for aggregate traffic measurement as described above. We do not share your data with any other third parties except as required by law.

Your workspace

FamilyPause workspaces are private by default. Only people you explicitly invite can access your workspace. You can remove workspace members, delete your workspace, or request deletion of all your data at any time by emailing hello@familypause.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days. That deletion includes FamilyPause account data and any stored Google Calendar connection tokens and email on our systems.

Children

FamilyPause is designed for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will notify you by email and update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of FamilyPause after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or data? Email us at hello@familypause.com. We read every message.
