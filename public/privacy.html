Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

FamilyPause helps families organize their week by turning conversations, notes, and transcripts into a shared plan that auto-syncs to their calendar. Your family's conversations are private. They always will be.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and first name. When you use FamilyPause we collect the content you provide, typed notes, pasted text, or recorded speech transcribed to text, along with the family member names, business names, and categories you add to your workspace. If you connect Google Calendar we store an access token and refresh token to sync events on your behalf. We also collect basic usage data such as session counts and feature interactions to improve the product.

How we use it

Your content is used for one purpose: to organize your week. Transcripts and notes you provide are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to extract appointments, tasks, reminders, and decisions. The extracted items are returned to your workspace and nothing else. We do not use your family's conversations to train AI models. We do not sell your data. We do not advertise to you based on your content. FamilyPause is and will always be ad-free.

Google Calendar integration

If you connect Google Calendar, FamilyPause uses your authorization token to create calendar events only for items you explicitly approve. We request only the minimum OAuth scope needed: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (create and manage events FamilyPause creates on your behalf). We do not read your existing calendar events. You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from Settings, which removes our access and deletes your stored tokens. FamilyPause's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site traffic, such as which pages are visited and how often. It is configured so it does not receive form entries, conversations, calendar information, names, email addresses, or other personal data. Page paths are sent without query strings. We do not use analytics for advertising.

Data storage and sharing

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure with encryption at rest. Workspace data, your plans, family member names, and session content, is visible only to members of your workspace. We share content with Anthropic solely for AI processing under their privacy terms. We use Google Analytics for aggregate traffic measurement as described above. We do not share your data with any other third parties except as required by law.

Your workspace

FamilyPause workspaces are private by default. Only people you explicitly invite can access your workspace. You can remove workspace members, delete your workspace, or request deletion of all your data at any time by emailing hello@familypause.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

FamilyPause is designed for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will notify you by email and update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of FamilyPause after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or data? Email us at hello@familypause.com. We read every message.
