Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

FamilyPause helps married couples organize their week from family meeting transcripts. We take your family's privacy seriously.

What we collect

Account email, family member names you enter, meeting transcripts you provide, and action items extracted from those transcripts.

How we use it

Transcripts are sent to our AI provider solely to extract action items and appointments for your workspace. We do not sell your data.

Sharing

Data in your workspace is shared with members you invite. We use Supabase for storage and Anthropic for AI processing under their respective privacy terms.
